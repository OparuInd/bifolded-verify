Engineering

Precision at Every Joint

Every Bifolded system is the result of obsessive engineering — where each hinge, seal, and surface is designed for decades of flawless performance and architectural beauty.

50K+ CYCLES

Military-Grade Hardware

316 stainless steel hinges and tracks rated for 50,000+ cycles. Each component is load-tested beyond residential requirements.

ASTM E330 RATED

Weather Integrity

Quadruple-sealed perimeter gaskets and thermal-break frames maintain envelope integrity through extreme conditions.

IoT READY

Smart Integration

Native compatibility with HomeKit, Google Home, and Alexa. Real-time position feedback and scheduling automation.

ZERO VISIBLE

Concealed Mechanics

All hardware — tracks, motors, and hinges — are precision-milled and hidden within the frame profile for a clean aesthetic.

U-VALUE 1.0

Thermal Performance

Multi-chamber frame profiles with polyamide thermal breaks achieve U-values as low as 1.0 W/m²K across the full door assembly.

< 45dB

Igea Quiet Run System

Igea Quiet Run motors operating below 45dB — quieter than a conversation. Soft-start and soft-stop prevent mechanical stress.
